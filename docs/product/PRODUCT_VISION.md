# N100M BUSINESS BUILDER
## Product vision, purpose and development blueprint

### 1. The vision

N100M Business Builder is a practical business-building application that helps people discover, start, develop and scale businesses using proven business principles, structured exercises, practical tools and guided decision-making.

It is inspired by Alex Hormozi's $100M book series and Acquisition.com business education materials.

The application should turn business education into business execution.

It is not supposed to be just:
- An ebook reader.
- A collection of business articles.
- A chatbot that gives generic business advice.
- A collection of disconnected worksheets.

It should be a structured workspace where users learn, make decisions, build business assets, test ideas and improve their businesses.

### 2. Who the application serves

There are two primary audiences.

**A. Aspiring entrepreneurs**

People who:
- Want to start a business but don't know what business to start.
- Have skills, experience, knowledge or resources but don't know how to turn them into a business.
- Need help identifying opportunities.
- Need to understand customers, markets, offers, pricing and customer acquisition.
- Want a practical path from idea to a functioning business.

**B. Existing business owners**

People who:
- Already operate a business.
- Want to improve their offers.
- Need more customers and better lead generation.
- Want to improve pricing, revenue and profitability.
- Need better business models and monetisation strategies.
- Want to build systems and scale.

The app must accommodate both audiences without assuming everyone is at the same stage.

### 3. The fundamental business-building journey

The application should guide users through this broad progression:

1. Understand yourself and your starting position.
2. Discover and evaluate business opportunities.
3. Select a business direction.
4. Understand the target market and customer.
5. Develop a compelling offer.
6. Determine how to attract leads.
7. Convert leads into paying customers.
8. Design revenue models and monetisation systems.
9. Deliver the product or service effectively.
10. Improve operations, profitability and customer experience.
11. Build systems and scale.
12. Review performance and continuously improve.

This is a recommended progression, not a rigid sequence. Existing business owners should be able to jump directly to the areas they need.

### 4. The main application modules

#### A. Dashboard

The user's business-building home.

It should eventually show:
- Current business or business idea.
- Current stage of development.
- Progress across important workbooks and exercises.
- Outstanding tasks.
- Recently updated work.
- Recommended next actions.
- Relevant business-building insights.

The dashboard should help users understand where they are and what to do next.

#### B. Business Discovery

Purpose: Help people identify a business opportunity that fits their capabilities and circumstances.

It should cover:

**Personal assessment**
- Skills.
- Work experience.
- Knowledge and expertise.
- Interests.
- Available resources.
- Startup capital.
- Equipment and assets.
- Available time.
- Networks and connections.
- Location.
- Personal preferences.
- Constraints and limitations.

**Market exploration**
- Problems people experience.
- Customer needs.
- Existing solutions.
- Underserved markets.
- Potential customer groups.
- Market demand.
- Competitive landscape.

**Opportunity matching**
- Match personal capabilities to market opportunities.
- Identify possible business directions.
- Evaluate feasibility.
- Compare opportunities.
- Identify assumptions requiring validation.
- Select or revisit a business direction.

The objective is not to promise that a questionnaire can identify a guaranteed profitable business. It should help users make better-informed decisions and validate their assumptions.

#### C. My Business

Purpose: Provide a central workspace for the user's chosen business.

It should eventually contain:
- Business overview.
- Business description.
- Target customers.
- Customer problems.
- Products and services.
- Value proposition.
- Business objectives.
- Business assumptions.
- Current priorities.
- Important business decisions.
- Progress and development history.

This should become the central reference point connecting the other modules.

#### D. Offers

Purpose: Help users create offers that customers have compelling reasons to buy.

Inspired primarily by $100M Offers.

Areas to cover:

- Identifying the customer's desired outcome.
- Understanding the customer's problems.
- Defining the value proposition.
- Improving the value equation.
- Increasing perceived value.
- Reducing effort and sacrifice.
- Increasing the likelihood of achieving the promised result.
- Reducing time to results.
- Differentiation.
- Pricing.
- Bonuses.
- Guarantees.
- Scarcity and urgency where genuine.
- Offer packaging.
- Offer testing and refinement.

Practical output: A documented, structured business offer that the user can actually take to market.

#### E. Leads

Purpose: Help users generate potential customers and turn attention into business opportunities.

Inspired primarily by $100M Leads.

Areas to cover:

- Understanding lead generation.
- Defining ideal prospects.
- Warm and cold audiences.
- Organic lead generation.
- Paid advertising.
- Content and social media.
- Referral systems.
- Affiliate marketing.
- Outreach.
- Lead magnets.
- Lead capture.
- Follow-up systems.
- Lead nurturing.
- Conversion tracking.

Practical output: A documented lead-generation strategy and executable campaigns.

#### F. Models

Purpose: Help users design how their businesses make money.

Inspired primarily by $100M Money Models.

Areas to cover:

- Attraction offers.
- Upsells.
- Downsells.
- Continuity offers.
- Customer lifetime value.
- Monetisation opportunities.
- Pricing structures.
- Revenue streams.
- Customer journey economics.
- Increasing transaction value.
- Improving repeat purchases.
- Improving profitability.

Practical output: A coherent revenue and monetisation structure for the business.

#### G. Scaling Roadmap

Purpose: Help established businesses develop the systems and capabilities required to grow.

The Acquisition.com Scaling Roadmap is an important external learning resource:

https://www.acquisition.com/training/scalingstart?hsLang=en

The application should provide:
- A link to the original course.
- Companion explanations and exercises.
- Practical implementation tools.
- Progress tracking.
- Business-specific application of the concepts.

Do not assume that third-party course content can be copied or embedded without permission. Preserve attribution and link to the original material.

Other Acquisition.com materials, including branding and GOATed Ads resources, may be incorporated as appropriate when supplied and reviewed.

#### H. Library

Purpose: Provide a central place for business-learning materials.

Potential contents:
- User-provided PDFs.
- Business books and reference materials.
- Templates.
- Worksheets.
- Frameworks.
- Notes.
- External learning links.
- Practical guides.

Include a built-in PDF viewer and support user-provided source materials.

#### I. Settings

Purpose: Manage the user's application experience.

Include:
- Light and dark themes.
- Currency preferences.
- Data import and export.
- Backup and restoration.
- Application preferences.
- AI configuration when implemented.

### 5. Learning and execution philosophy

The application should combine learning with action.

Every important concept should ideally include:

1. A clear explanation.
2. Why it matters.
3. A practical example.
4. Relevant source material.
5. A guided exercise or workbook.
6. A practical output the user can use.
7. A way to review and improve that output.

Avoid turning the app into a collection of long theoretical lessons.

Users should be able to learn something and immediately apply it to their own businesses.

The app should provide a recommended progression while allowing users to explore freely.

### 6. Workbooks and practical tools

Workbooks are a fundamental part of the application.

The workbook engine should support:
- Short and long text.
- Numbers.
- Single-choice selections.
- Multiple-choice selections.
- Checkboxes.
- Required and optional questions.
- Sections.
- Validation.
- Progress indicators.
- Autosaving.
- Recovery from failed saves.
- Persistent responses.

Over time, build specialised workbooks for the different modules.

Examples:
- Business self-assessment.
- Business opportunity evaluation.
- Customer avatar development.
- Customer problem analysis.
- Offer creation.
- Value equation analysis.
- Pricing.
- Lead-generation planning.
- Advertising campaign planning.
- Revenue model design.
- Business scaling assessment.

The workbook engine should remain reusable rather than implementing each exercise as an unrelated custom form.

### 7. AI assistance

AI should be an optional enhancement, not a dependency.

Potential capabilities:
- Help users understand concepts.
- Guide users through exercises.
- Critique business ideas.
- Evaluate offers.
- Suggest improvements.
- Analyse user-provided materials.
- Help interpret business information.
- Generate drafts and working documents.

AI should use the user's actual business context where appropriate.

The core application must remain functional without AI.

Do not introduce mandatory AI subscriptions or cloud dependencies into the foundation.

### 8. Technical and data principles

**Local-first**
- No mandatory login.
- No mandatory cloud.
- User data stored locally.
- One business workspace per installation for v1.

**Data safety**
- Use IndexedDB behind the existing storage abstraction.
- Protect against accidental data loss.
- Handle failed writes safely.
- Provide backup and restoration.
- Treat data portability as a core feature.

**Obsidian compatibility — non-negotiable**

Support:
- Markdown import/export.
- YAML frontmatter.
- Wikilinks.
- Tags.
- Callouts where practical.
- Relative attachment paths.
- Obsidian-compatible folder structures.
- Whole-project exports rather than one enormous Markdown document.
- Round-trip import/export without losing metadata or relationships.

Users should not become trapped in a proprietary data format.

Initial integration should be file-based Markdown or ZIP vault import/export. Direct live Obsidian integration can come later.

**Platform**
- React 19.
- TypeScript.
- Vite.
- Capacitor Android.
- IndexedDB.
- Vitest.
- GitHub Actions.

### 9. User interface and brand

The application must be mobile-first and responsive.

Mobile navigation:
- Dashboard.
- Offers.
- Leads.
- Models.

Other sections are accessible through More.

Tablet and desktop layouts should adapt appropriately.

Devices below 390px need a deliberately compact mobile experience.

Brand palette:

- Off-white: #F7F4EF
- Secondary off-white: #EFEAE0
- Black: #1A1A1A
- Soft black: #2B2B2B
- Burgundy: #7A1F1F
- Burgundy hover: #8F2727
- Gold: #B8923E
- Readable gold text: #8A6A1F

Use an elegant, mature, professional appearance. Avoid generic SaaS styling and unrelated colours.

### 10. What success looks like

A successful N100M Business Builder user should be able to:

- Understand their starting position.
- Identify and evaluate a business opportunity.
- Develop a clear business direction.
- Understand their customers.
- Create a compelling offer.
- Establish a customer acquisition strategy.
- Design a revenue model.
- Organise their business knowledge and plans.
- Execute practical business-building activities.
- Track progress.
- Improve their business over time.
- Develop the systems needed to scale.

The ultimate goal is to help users move from business uncertainty to informed decisions, practical execution and sustainable business growth.

### 11. Product development principles

Build in stages.

Prioritise:
1. A reliable foundation.
2. Useful, complete workflows.
3. Data safety.
4. Excellent mobile usability.
5. Practical business outputs.
6. Consistency across modules.
7. Long-term maintainability.

Do not build features merely because they sound impressive.

Do not add unnecessary complexity.

Do not make AI a prerequisite.

Do not sacrifice data portability.

Do not confuse a large number of features with a useful product.

**The guiding principle: N100M Business Builder should help people build real businesses, not just consume business information.**
